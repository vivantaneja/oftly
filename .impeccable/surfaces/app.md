# Application surface
Mode: Operate. Primary target: src/App.tsx. Related: src/styles.css.
The learner opens Today, studies due cards, or imports/creates a deck. Library, progress, and settings support that loop.
## Direction contract
THESIS: A focused study desk with one obvious next action and an orderly deck library. Avoid gamified dashboards and widget grids.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white canvas, pale eucalyptus navigation, dark pine actions, graphite type. Familiar sans typography, aligned rows, compact controls and generous study space.
STORY: See what is ready, retrieve an answer, reveal and rate, leave knowing what comes next. First-run content offers import, create, or a clearly labeled sample deck.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow left navigation rail; a wide work area headed Today; a study action and explanatory sentence; a table-like deck list. A narrow supporting weekly activity strip uses only actual review counts. On phones the navigation becomes a compact bottom bar.
FORM: User-pinned familiar Anki/Quizlet workflow with quiet product UI. Seed unavailable: launcher requires an unavailable installed engine. seed: none (unavailable).
SIGNATURE: The question stays in place while the answer unfolds beneath it, preserving the learner's context and then exposing meaningful rating labels with actual Anki intervals.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
