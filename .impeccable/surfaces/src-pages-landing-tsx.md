# Guided first visit

Mode: Operate. Existing Oftly identity; code-led refinement, no new visual world or comp.

## User authority — 11 November 2026

The user rejected the tall scroll page, scrolling buttons, obscure subject choices, card-count/deadline questions and one-hour ceiling. They asked for an active, designated path with much less text/options and support for people studying eight hours a day. This supersedes the prior long-form 2/3 sketch combination.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — Understand one card by using it, then make a simple study plan.

**OWN-WORLD** — Preserve Oftly's warm canvas, ink, fonts and existing icons. Bring the entry flow closer to the quiet study application.

**STORY** — Reveal → recall response → study setting → current method → hours/minutes → a short suggested split → optional account or guest study. Each screen has one task. Back preserves answers and a reload resumes setup.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Compact brand/sign-in header, modest heading, a single question and Reveal answer. It fits 320–1440px viewports at ordinary text size without requiring a marketing scroll.

**FORM** — 540px task column, flat choice rows, 44px controls, factual progress, no nested cards or arbitrary numeric efficacy claims. Three setting options and three methods; no card totals, horizons or long subject list. Daily time accepts 5 minutes to 24 hours. The suggested allocation never limits actual studying. Only the answer reveal animates; reduced motion disables it.

**FINISH** — Inspect desktop, tablet and phone states; verify keyboard/large text, eight-hour input, plan persistence and account handoff. Fresh independent finish review includes the user's criticism. Existing design tokens and assets stay unchanged; no new rasters ship.
