# Landing and account onboarding

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — Follow one card as the learner recalls, rates and returns; connect that experience to a practical study rhythm.

**OWN-WORLD** — Existing Oftly index-paper canvas, ink, marigold recall accents, Bricolage Grotesque display type and Atkinson Hyperlegible UI. Public explanation is expressive; the working study surface remains quiet.

**STORY** — A centered invitation and live sample card lead into a scroll-linked timeline. The memory curve shows an illustrative change, then a short questionnaire gives transparent workload planning. Google or email-link sign-in saves private cards and progress; guests can continue locally.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Broad, centered headline above one prominent flashcard. An expanding timeline anchors the composition. Header offers a direct sign-in; a clear study-plan action avoids forcing the explanatory scroll.

**FORM** — User chose “2/3”: combine Follow a single card (grounded list position 4) with Watch memory change (position 1). Follow a single card owns the opening composition; memory curves inhabit a later ink chapter. Seed reference seed:6c182fe82fb2237c. Code-led; no image-generation tooling. One linked scroll scene, no scroll hijacking, reduced-motion static sequence. No invented benefits or efficacy percentages. Estimate is a configurable workload budget, not a forecast of learning or grades.
