# Guided first visit

Mode: Persuade at the opening, then Operate for setup. Existing Oftly identity; code-led refinement using the original landing as the explicit style reference, no new visual world or comp.

## User authority — 11 November 2026

The user rejected the tall scroll page, scrolling buttons, obscure subject choices, card-count/deadline questions and one-hour ceiling. They asked for an active, designated path with much less text/options and support for people studying eight hours a day. A subsequent request asks to tie back into the old landing style. Restore its expressive headline, paper sample card and ink-and-gold recall illustration while retaining the shorter active path and simpler questions; do not restore the entire scroll page.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — Understand one card by using it, then make a simple study plan.

**OWN-WORLD** — Preserve Oftly's warm canvas, ink, fonts and existing icons. Restore the original first impression through centered editorial type, a single paper flashcard and a short ink-and-gold explanation after the recall response. Keep the study application quiet.

**STORY** — Reveal → recall response → study setting → current method → hours/minutes → a short suggested split → optional account or guest study. Each screen has one task. Back preserves answers and a reload resumes setup.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Brand/sign-in header, the original “One card. A longer-lasting memory.” headline, one short description, a paper sample card and Reveal answer. It fits 320–1440px viewports at ordinary text size. Rating reveals a compact explanation and finite curve animation before setup.

**FORM** — Up to 1040px opening with centered 480px card; 540px task column for setup, flat choice rows, 44px controls, factual progress, no nested cards or arbitrary numeric efficacy claims. Three setting options and three methods; no card totals, horizons or long subject list. Daily time accepts 5 minutes to 24 hours. The suggested allocation never limits actual studying. Answer reveal and the rating-triggered curve animate once; reduced motion disables both. The curve changes with the rating and is explicitly illustrative, without fabricated day counts.

**FINISH** — Inspect desktop, tablet and phone states; verify keyboard/large text, eight-hour input, plan persistence and account handoff. Fresh independent finish review includes the user's criticism. Existing design tokens and assets stay unchanged; no new rasters ship.
