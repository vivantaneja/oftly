Privacy & data

Oftly is a flashcard app built by Vivan and Ashvant. Guest study works without an account. Account sync is optional.

On this device

Cards, sources, media and review history are stored in this browser. We also keep your study-planner answers and preferences. Clearing site storage can erase unsynced work. The app caches its public files for offline use.

When you sign in

Supabase provides authentication and stores your library in its Ireland region. Google sign-in shares your email and basic profile with Supabase. Email links are delivered through Resend. Your library is private to your account, protected by access rules. This is not end-to-end encryption: service operators can administer the backend.

Sync and sign-out

Each account has a separate offline library in this browser. Signing out hides it but leaves the offline copy here. On a shared device, sign out and clear this site’s storage. Conflicting edits are preserved for you to resolve; download both versions before choosing.

Export and deletion

Download your cards, history and media from Settings → Backups. Account deletion is available in Account and removes the server library and sign-in identity. Other devices may retain offline copies until their site data is cleared.

No advertising or analytics

Oftly does not include advertising trackers or analytics. Hosting and authentication providers process operational logs, such as request IP addresses, for delivery and security. Your planner answers are used for the study plan, not for advertising.
